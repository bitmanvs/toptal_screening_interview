# Next Step — Technical Interview (Toptal)

## What happens

Schedule a **technical interview** to validate your coding-test results. Treat it like a **client meeting** — professional tone, clear communication, working setup.

## Before the call — checklist

| Item | Action |
|------|--------|
| Dev environment | **"Hello World"** (or equivalent) **ready and tested** — IDE, runtime, project opens, run/debug works |
| Internet | Stable connection; close heavy downloads/streaming during the call |
| Web camera | Working, good lighting, camera at eye level |
| Screen sharing | Test share (full screen or window) in Zoom/Meet/etc. before the interview |
| Audio | Mic tested; quiet room; headphones if possible |

## During the interview

- Speak clearly; explain thought process when asked
- Share screen only when needed; keep desktop/workspace professional (no clutter, no sensitive tabs)
- Have the same language/tooling you used for the test available

## Rejection risk

**Failure to present a professional online presence** (broken camera, bad connection, unprepared environment, messy screen share) can cause **application rejection**.
